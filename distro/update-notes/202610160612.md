# Mentor update: the recipe switch only switches off

## What is new

In the Recipes app, the switch next to a recipe now plainly says that off means switched off, with everything kept, so on brings it back. For a widget, the switch now works at once by itself, without waiting for the assistant.

## Why

- mentor-recipes: the switch's tooltip said "Off: taken away" and a switched-off site recipe said "taken away again", though the switch never removes anything; now "Off: switched off, its files kept" and "switched off".
- A recipe whose "Touches" row names only a GNOME Shell extension in the home folder is switched by the app itself (mentor_recipes.only_extension finds the installed extension by the part of its name before the @; mentor_tools.extension_set turns it off or on; recipe_used notes it). Other recipes still go to the assistant, which is told to keep every file.

Changed: mentor-recipes, mentor_recipes.py, RECIPES.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- In the Recipes app, the switch's tooltip says "Off: switched off, its files kept".
