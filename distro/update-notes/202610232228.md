# Mentor update: one working sign in the chat, a clearer gear

## What is new

While Mentor works on an answer, the chat shows one sign at a time: the turning gear on the steps card while steps run, and the small ring only before the first step. The steps card's title and gear are now bright instead of faded, and the gear turns more slowly and smoothly.

## Why

The user saw the ring and the gear at once, found the card muffled and the gear too fast. In `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat`: `show_waiting` hides the ring (`self.ring`) while a steps card runs and shows it again when the card ends; `.steps .steps-header:disabled` no longer takes GNOME's half-dimming; the gear is 18 px in `#9cc8ff`; `GEAR_STEPS` 32 and `GEAR_TICK` 120 ms make a turn about four seconds. ASSISTANT.md, "The steps card", says it all. The chat takes the change when it starts again (after the restart that comes with the update).

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- Ask something that needs a step: the ring shows while it thinks, then only the gear turns on the card, and a tick comes when the answer is there.
