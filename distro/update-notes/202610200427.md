# Mentor update: Show Apps in your window colors

## What is new

- Show Apps (the nine dots) and the search take the tones of your window colors: with a light set the whole view is light, with its folders, search box and results a little deeper, and the names dark; with a dark set, its dark tone. GNOME's own colors keep GNOME's look.
- OpenSnitch's own window is no longer listed among the apps: Mentor is its interface.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: with a light set, Show Apps stood black under a light taskbar, and the guard's package added an app of its own to the grid. The extension's part `menu colors` (`overview()`); `/usr/local/share/applications/opensnitch_ui.desktop`. DESKTOP.md, "Window colors"; SECURITY.md, "Programs going online".
