# Mentor update: a Themes tab, accent colors, the pointer, and no surprise restarts

## What is new

- Mentor Settings has a **Themes** tab: Window colors and the Pointer, out of the Settings tab.
- **Accent** sets beside Dark and Light (Blue, Teal, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red, Pink, Purple, Slate): title bars, the taskbar, switches and buttons in that color, as Windows' "accent color on title bars and taskbar". GNOME's accent color follows.
- **Pointer**: black, white or the accent color, and a size slider.
- **No surprise restarts**: Update while the assistant works says so and offers "Update when it's done"; GNOME's Log Out, Restart and Power Off say "Mentor Assistant: Still working" first.
- **Show Apps** lists every app, Files, Chromium and Console included, and the taskbar's text stays readable there on a light set. Dragging a pinned app in Show Apps no longer unpins it.
- The box over a program and the assistant's name in notifications use the simpler owl; Mentor Settings and Recipes open a little wider.

## Why

- The user's wishes, 2026-10-19. `mentor_colors.py` (`ACCENT_SETS`, title bars painted for them), `mentor_pointer.py` (Adwaita's pointer recolored into `~/.local/share/icons/Mentor-pointer-*`; `mentor pointer`), mentor-settings (Themes tab, `mentor settings themes`), mentor-chat (`hold_session`: GNOME's session inhibitor while the assistant works; `install_update` waits; follows the accent color for the pointer), extension parts `pinned apps in the grid`, `taskbar color` (dark text in the overview), `menu colors` (grey menus for accent sets). DESKTOP.md "Window colors", "Pointer", "Restarts while Mentor works"; SETTINGS.md; UPDATES.md; TOOLS.md.
