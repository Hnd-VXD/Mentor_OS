# Mentor update: the gear turns while Mentor works, and a narrow bell when it is empty

## What is new

- The gear on the card of steps turns while Mentor is working on your request, and becomes a tick when the answer is there. Before, it stood still.
- When the bell has no cards, it opens narrow, with the three "More" buttons one under the other, instead of a wide, mostly empty box.

## Why

- mentor-chat: Mentor has GNOME's animations off (enable-animations=false, for speed in the VM), and GTK then leaves CSS animations still, so the gear's turning animation never ran. It is now turned by a timer, a sixteenth of a turn every 90 ms (GEAR_STEPS, the classes turn-0 to turn-15), only while the conversation is on screen.
- mentor-chat: the scrolling area of the bell's cards is hidden when there are none (it kept its space before), and the "More" buttons stand one under the other then.

Changed: mentor-chat, ASSISTANT.md, SUGGESTIONS.md.
