# Mentor update: the assistant finds settings, programs, files and its own instructions by what you say

## What is new

- Ask in your own words, "make the letters bigger", "the screen goes dark too soon", and the assistant finds the right setting itself instead of guessing.
- "Open something to write a letter", "what opens this file?": it finds the right program, in your language too.
- "Find the photos from August", "the letter about the party", "what fills my disk?": it finds your files by what is in them, by date or by size.
- When something of Mentor's stops working, it goes straight to the right repair instructions.
- `mentor verify` no longer lists six harmless Python cache files as missing.

## Why

- Four new tools in `mentor_tools.py` (TOOLS.md has a section for each): `settings_find` (GSettings keys by their descriptions, and with `changed` the keys this system set away from how they came), `docs_find` (the section of /etc/mentor/*.md and ~/.local/share/mentor/notes.md that answers a question), `apps_find` (installed applications by what they do, in the launchers' own languages, and with `opens` what opens a file), `files_find` (the home folder through GNOME's LocalSearch index: words in the name or inside, kind, date taken or changed, folder, size). AGENT.md tells you when to use each.
- Under the heading of each section where something is mended, the instruction files now have a line "In the user's words: …", what people say when they need that section; `docs_find` weighs it like a heading.
- build.sh left Python's caches (`__pycache__`) out of MANIFEST.sha256: every Mentor's `mentor verify` called six `.pyc` files missing. This update's list of files has none, so those lines go.
- If the user says one of these finds the wrong thing: the tool's section in TOOLS.md, "How it is made", says how it ranks and where its word lists are. If `files_find` says LocalSearch could not be asked: `localsearch status`, and `file_search` meanwhile.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built (no `__pycache__` lines).
- `mentor call settings_find '{"query": "text scaling"}'` gives `org.gnome.desktop.interface text-scaling-factor` first.
- `mentor call docs_find '{"query": "chat does not open"}'` starts `Best: /etc/mentor/ASSISTANT.md`.
- `mentor call apps_find '{"query": "calculator"}'` gives `org.gnome.Calculator`.
- `mentor call files_find '{"sort": "newest", "results": 3}'` lists three files (or says none fits on a new system).
