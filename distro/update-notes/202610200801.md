# Mentor update: History in a window of its own

## What is new

- History opens as a window like any program's: minimize, maximize, close, drag, resize, and an icon in the taskbar. Esc clears the search, then closes it.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: History, drawn inside the desktop window, could only be closed. `show_history` in `mentor-chat` (an `Adw.Window`); the extension leaves windows titled as in `PLAIN_TITLES` ("History") as normal windows, never the desktop. DESKTOP.md, "The desktop cannot be moved".

## Step by step

1. Nothing to do: the restart after the update loads the new shell extension. The chat and the extension must come together: with the old extension, the new History would be taken for the desktop.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- Status of `org.mentor.Shell` says `assistant_on_desktop` true after opening History.
