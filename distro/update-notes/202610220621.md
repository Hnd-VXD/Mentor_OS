# Mentor update: the picture menu opens at once

## What is new

- The menu after a screenshot or a Windows Print Screen opens much sooner, however big the picture is, and the desktop no longer freezes for a moment while it opens.
- Edit, Copy text and WebP in that menu now work even when the Windows picture was already replaced by a later copy.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-22: the menu after Print Screen came late, the later the bigger the picture. Measured: `mentor-clipboard` read the picture from the port 4 KB at a time, 64 reads every 50 ms, into a growing `bytes` (a 4.7 MB snip took 0.3 to 3.5 s to arrive); it also decoded the whole picture just to check it; the shell extension's `pictureMenu` decoded it again on the shell's thread before the menu opened (with glycin even `get_file_info` decodes it all).
- Changed: `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-clipboard` (`read_host` reads until the port is empty into a `bytearray`, the rest of a long line 5 ms on; `on_message` checks only the PNG header) and the shell extension (`pictureMenu` reads the size from the PNG header, keeps the file's bytes, decodes the pixels only for Copy smaller and the JPGs). Now about 0.15 s from the picture arriving to the menu. DESKTOP.md, "Pictures from Windows" and "The menu after a picture".
- A newer Mentor.exe (published the same day) also gets the picture from Windows sooner; the time left before the menu is mostly Windows handing the snip over.

## Step by step

1. Nothing to do: the restart after the update loads the new shell extension and starts `mentor-clipboard` again.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `pgrep -af 'lib/mentor/mentor-clipboard$'` shows one line.
