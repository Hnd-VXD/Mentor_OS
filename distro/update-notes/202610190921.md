# Mentor update: the key comes back by itself; folders show all their apps

## What is new

- When you open NVIDIA's page to get a key, the page no longer leaves the key box hidden: as soon as you copy the key there, Mentor comes back to the front with the key already in the box. Press Save.
- A folder in Show Apps shows the apps that are pinned to the taskbar in its little picture too.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19. The key card is on the desktop, and the page opened over it. "Open NVIDIA's page" now asks the shell to watch the clipboard (`AwaitCopy` in the extension, the shape in `KEY_SHAPES`); a copied key brings the desktop to the front (as Super+A), and `take_copied_key` in `mentor-chat` puts it in the box. ENGINES.md.
- A new folder's picture showed only the one app of three that was not pinned. Each time a folder lists its apps, its picture is drawn again (part `pinned apps in the grid`). DESKTOP.md.

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: it loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `gdbus introspect --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell | grep AwaitCopy` shows the method.
