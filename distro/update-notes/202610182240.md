# Mentor update: window buttons, matched light and dark, sturdier repair docs

## What is new

- The buttons at the right of every window are small colored squares: minimize yellow, maximize green, close red at the far right. The same size in GNOME's programs, Chromium and Electron programs (Bitwarden); the sign shows under the pointer. Mentor's own dialogs (a picture, History) close with the red one.
- While one of Mentor's window color sets is in use, light and dark stay the set's: GNOME Settings' Style buttons are put back at once with a short notice ("Light and dark come with your window colors"), and the Dark Style switch is hidden from the quick menu. With GNOME's own sets the style is free.
- The box over a program (Alt+Space): with nothing written, its round button is a × that puts it away.
- The instruction files the assistant repairs Mentor from were tested by breaking Mentor on purpose 14 ways and having a small model fix it from the files alone; every gap found is filled. The build now refuses to pack a Mentor that has a part its files do not explain.

## Why

- The user's wishes, 2026-10-18 and 19: Mac-like lights at the right, squares, a bit bigger; Style in Settings "messes with our themes"; an × in the empty box.
- `mentor_colors.py`: `LIGHTS`, `light()`, `BUTTONS_GTK4`, `BUTTONS_GTK3`, `buttons()` (a block of its own in `~/.config/gtk-4.0/gtk.css` and `gtk-3.0/gtk.css`, written by the assistant at every start); `apply` now changes the style last. Extension part `keep style`. mentor-chat: `light-close`, `closes_ask`, `show_action`. DESKTOP.md "Window buttons", "Window colors"; ASKBAR.md; REBUILD.md "When Mentor itself changes"; FREEZES.md, UPDATES.md, TOOLS.md. `build/check-docs.py` in the build.

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: its new part loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `grep -c "BEGIN Mentor window buttons" ~/.config/gtk-4.0/gtk.css` says 1 once the assistant has started.
- `gdbus call --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell --method org.mentor.Shell.Status` shows `"keep style":"ok"`.
