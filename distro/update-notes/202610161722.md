# Mentor update: Mentor's self-check covers updates too

## What is new

Mentor's own check of itself, which runs after you log in, now also makes sure Mentor keeps looking for its updates and may install them. If something there stopped working, you get a yellow card instead of quietly missing updates. The QEMU window of Mentor on Windows shows the owl and the name Mentor (with the new Mentor.exe).

## Why

- mentor (doctor): three new checks: the daily look (mentor-update-check.timer), a look in the last two days or, failing that, a look asked for the way the Update button does it (the polkit rule; never a password box), and mentor-taskbar-quiet.service.
- mentor_update.py: every look writes a line to /var/log/mentor/updates.log ("looked: …"), also when the card could not be left.

Changed: mentor, mentor_update.py, UPDATES.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor doctor` shows ok for "the daily look for Mentor updates" and "Mentor looked for an update in the last two days".
- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
