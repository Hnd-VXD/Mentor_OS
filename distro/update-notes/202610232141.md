# Mentor update: the guard on programs going online, made tighter and kinder

## What is new

Apps from Flathub, Steam and AppImages now count as known only when they really come from there, so a program cannot slip online by sitting in a folder with such a name. Flatpak apps written in Python are no longer held back by mistake. Unblock in Mentor Settings takes effect at once, and a program you allowed also works when started through a shortcut. Cards about programs waiting to go online come back after Mentor restarts, and common programs such as Flatpak and the browser work on their very first try.

## Why

Found by testing update 23.10.26.02 (SECURITY.md, "Programs going online", and "When it is broken" 6j).

- `mentor_guard.origin` judged app stores by words in the path: any folder named Steam, steamapps or flatpak, or a self-made `/tmp/.mount_x`, counted as known and went online without asking; the watch for unusual programs counted it trusted. Now: AppImages while mounted (FUSE), Flatpak under its real folders, Steam under its own folder or a library it lists in `libraryfolders.vdf`.
- mentor-netguard: Flatpak sandboxes are looked at inside (`/proc/PID/root/.flatpak-info`); the command line comes from `/proc/PID/cmdline` (OpenSnitch sends it as typed); bare names are looked up on the search path; links are resolved; Unblock and root's Block go through the guard so the daemon is told; the answer socket reads the whole message; common online programs (`EARLY`) and the AI engines get their known rule when the guard starts; no rule for an empty command line.
- mentor-chat: waiting programs get their card back when the chat starts (`remind_waiting`); grouped cards tell same-named programs apart (`mentor_guard.net_names`).
- If a game, AppImage or Flatpak app is asked about though it came from there: SECURITY.md, 6j.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built; `mentor security` has no line "NOT WORKING".
- `systemctl is-active mentor-netguard opensnitchd` says active twice.
- `ls /etc/opensnitchd/rules | grep known` lists, among others, flatpak, pacman and curl.
