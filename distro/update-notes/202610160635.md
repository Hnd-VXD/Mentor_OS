# Mentor update: every card can be talked about, and update cards stay quiet

## What is new

Every card Mentor puts in the chat now has a button that takes it into a conversation, so you can ask what it means. Update cards no longer pop up at the top of the screen: they wait quietly in the chat. And switching a recipe off in the Recipes app now asks you first.

## Why

- mentor-chat: a card with no request of its own gets "Talk about it" (an update card without notes: "What's new?"); it goes to a new conversation like Let Mentor look. Cards marked quiet (`mentor notice --quiet`) bring no GNOME pop-up.
- mentor_update.py: the updater's cards are quiet. mentor, mentor_tools.py: `--quiet` / `quiet`.
- mentor-recipes: switching a recipe off asks "Switch off …? It stops working until you switch it on again here. Nothing is deleted."; Cancel leaves it as it was.

Changed: mentor-chat, mentor-recipes, mentor, mentor_tools.py, mentor_update.py, ASSISTANT.md, UPDATES.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- A card that used to have only Dismiss now also has Talk about it.
