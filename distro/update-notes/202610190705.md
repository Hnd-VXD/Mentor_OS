# Mentor update: the desktop stays still

## What is new

- Dragging the dark space around a picture's preview, or around History, no longer moves the desktop with the mouse and makes it jitter: it stays still.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19. A dialog of the chat is a window attached to the desktop, and a drag around it is a move of the desktop that the window manager carries out to the end. The extension's `_holdStill` (on `grab-op-begin` for the desktop window) now shifts the picture of the desktop and its dialogs back by as much as it moved, in the same frame, and `_letGo` puts it back in its place when the drag ends. `Status` counts `desktop_drags_held` and `desktop_put_back` (should stay 0). DESKTOP.md "The desktop cannot be moved, hidden or closed by mistake"; ASSISTANT.md (attachments).

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: it loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- After a drag beside an open History, `gdbus call --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell --method org.mentor.Shell.Status` shows `desktop_drags_held` above 0 and `desktop_put_back` 0.
