# Mentor update: programs ask before going online; places with city and country

## What is new

The first time a program Mentor does not know tries to go online, it now stays offline and a card asks you: "NAME wants to go online", where to (with the city and country), and Block or Allow. If you do not answer, it stays offline. The chat's menu (top left) has two new lists: Network…, with every program that asked and what you answered, and Important warnings…, with the red cards shown; anything in them can be sent to Mentor again. Several programs at once give one card, not one each. Chat messages that start with a dash or a slash are now sent to the agent as they are.

## Why

Before, an unknown program was let through and only reported afterwards (SECURITY.md, "Programs going online", which now says how everything works and what to do when it is broken, 6a to 6i).

- mentor-netguard answers every question from OpenSnitch at once: unknown programs get a waiting rule (`mentor-wait-*.json`); Allow and Block come from the card, Network… or Mentor Settings through `/run/mentor-netguard/answer.sock`, no password. Changes are also sent to the running daemon over its Notifications stream, as OpenSnitch 1.7.2 stops reading its rules' folder a while after it starts.
- While the guard answers, OpenSnitch's `DefaultAction` is deny (it refuses what it cannot ask about); when the guard stops or fails it is set back to allow, so the internet never depends on it. A guard that keeps crashing is given up after about a minute (`mentor security` says NOT WORKING).
- Scripts are judged as the file they run, however they are started (relative paths, `./script`, `env`); code given as text (`python3 -c`, stdin, `bash -c`) is its own entry, "python3 command".
- Known programs are allowed for good by `mentor-known-*.json` rules (asked once).
- Places: DB-IP's free City database, fetched monthly by `mentor-geo.timer` (about 60 MB download, 127 MB on disk), looked up on this computer only; package `python-maxminddb` comes with this update.
- New: `mentor_geo.py`, `mentor-geo.service`/`.timer`, `mentor-globe-symbolic.svg`, Network… and Important warnings… (`show_network`, `show_warnings`, `~/.local/state/mentor/cards.jsonl`). Changed: mentor-netguard and its unit, mentor_guard.py, mentor-watch, mentor_tools.py, mentor-chat (also: messages starting with "-" or "/" reach every engine as text: ENGINES.md), mentor_repair.py, mentor-settings, `mentor settings security`, the extension (`PLAIN_TITLES`).
- If the user says a program they want cannot go online: `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-netguard list` (waiting means never answered); SECURITY.md, "When it is broken", 6c and 6f.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built; `mentor security` has no line "NOT WORKING".
- `systemctl is-active mentor-netguard opensnitchd` says active twice; `grep DefaultAction /etc/opensnitchd/default-config.json` says deny.
- The chat's menu (top left) shows Network… and Important warnings….
- A few minutes after the restart, `python3 /usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor_geo.py 8.8.8.8` names a city and a country.
