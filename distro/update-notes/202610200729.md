# Mentor update: one picture menu at a time

## What is new

- Two pictures from Windows in a row no longer leave two menus open: the newer picture's menu closes the older one.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: two Print Screens left two menus on the screen. The extension's `pictureMenu` keeps the open menu (`shownPictureMenu`) and closes it before a new one opens. DESKTOP.md, "The menu after a picture".

## Step by step

1. Nothing to do: the restart after the update loads the new shell extension.
