# Mentor update: programs may start from temporary folders again

## What is new

- OpenCode, and other programs that unpack a part of themselves into a temporary folder, start again.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: OpenCode stopped opening after 19.10.26.13 ("Failed to initialize OpenTUI render library … failed to map segment from shared object"): Bun unpacks a library into /tmp, and /tmp had been made noexec. That protection only stopped a program put in /tmp by path, and the watch for unusual programs already sees one there; the user chose to take it back. `mentor-noexec` and its service are removed; `mentor-enable` removes its switch-on link. SECURITY.md, "Temporary folders hold programs too (on purpose)".

## Step by step

1. The restart that installs this update starts without the protection; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `findmnt -no OPTIONS --target /tmp` has no `noexec`.
