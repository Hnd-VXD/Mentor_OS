# Mentor update: window colors

## What is new

- Mentor Settings has **Window colors**: 20 calm color sets, 10 dark (Slate, Navy, Forest, Espresso…) and 10 light (Cream, Soft yellow, Sand, Sage…), each with two matching tones like GNOME's own. Click one: Mentor Settings changes at once, other programs when they are opened again.
- The **taskbar** and the **shell's menus** (quick settings, calendar and notifications, keyboard menu) take the set's tones too, at once. In a light set the taskbar's nine dots turn dark so they can be seen.
- Switching **Dark Style** by hand against the set gives the taskbar and the menus GNOME's own colors back, so their text is always readable; back to the set's style, its tones return.
- The chat's own dialogs (a picture, History, a suggestion) follow the set too.
- Dragging the title of a picture opened in the chat no longer makes the chat flicker.
- You can also ask Mentor: "make my windows cream".

## Why

- The user asked for window colors with tones, professional and not punchy, and for the taskbar, its dots, Mentor's dialogs and the shell's menus to follow (2026-10-18).
- `mentor_colors.py` writes libadwaita's documented named colors into the user's own GTK stylesheets (`~/.config/gtk-4.0/gtk.css`, `gtk-3.0/gtk.css`) inside a block of Mentor's, sets GNOME's light or dark style to match, and writes the tones to `~/.config/mentor/taskbar-color` (four fields). `mentor colors [NAME]` for the agent.
- The extension's parts `taskbar color` (Dash to Panel's `trans-bg-color`) and `menu colors` (a stylesheet loaded on the shell's theme) follow that file and `color-scheme`. `stylesheet-light.css` makes the dots dark in light style; `stylesheet-dark.css` is nearly empty so the shell swaps the two live.
- mentor-chat: `follow_colors` puts a new set on its dialogs at once; `keep_still` stops a drag on an empty part of a dialog from moving the desktop window.
- DESKTOP.md, "Window colors" (with the check to do after a new major GNOME), ASSISTANT.md, SETTINGS.md, REBUILD.md.

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: the new parts load at the next login. If the user has no windows open, nothing else is needed; the restart that installs this update logs in again.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `mentor colors` lists 20 sets.
- `gdbus call --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell --method org.mentor.Shell.Status` shows `"taskbar color":"ok"` and `"menu colors":"ok"`.
