# Mentor update: Flatpak programs in Mentor's colors

## What is new

- Programs from Flathub (Google Chrome, for example) get Mentor's colored window buttons and window colors, as other programs do. A program already open shows them when opened again.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: Google Chrome from Flathub had GNOME's plain buttons. Flatpak programs cannot see `~/.config` unless allowed; now every one may read `~/.config/gtk-3.0` and `~/.config/gtk-4.0`, read only: `mentor_colors.flatpak_style()` (the user's own, at every login), `mentor-enable` (those installed for everyone).
