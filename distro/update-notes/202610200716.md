# Mentor update: a menu for each picture

## What is new

- After a picture from Windows (Print Screen, Snipping Tool) and after Mentor's own screenshots, a small menu opens where the pointer is: Copy smaller (75%, 50%, 30%), Copy in format (PNG, JPG or WebP), Copy text (the words in the picture), Save (a JPG in Pictures/Screenshots), Save as…, Edit.
- The menu for a picture from Windows closes by itself after 20 seconds. "Don't show this after Print Screen" turns it off; Mentor Settings, More choices, "A menu after Print Screen" turns it on again.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: a full screenshot pasted into a web page is large; a smaller copy, or a JPG or WebP, is lighter. The extension's `PictureMenu` / `HostPictureMenu`, `mentor-clipboard` (`picture_menu`), `mentor save-picture`, `mentor capture-text --file`; the setting `picture_menu`; `python-pillow` for WebP. DESKTOP.md, "The menu after a picture".
