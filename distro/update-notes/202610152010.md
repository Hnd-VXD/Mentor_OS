# Mentor update: Mentor asks before it restarts for an update

## What is new

Installing an update restarts Mentor. From now on Mentor says so on the update card, and asks you first when you press Update, so you can save your work in other apps before it restarts.

## Why

Until now the restart came a second or two after Update was pressed, with the only warning shown after the press (15.10.26.02 surprised its user). Now:

- The update card says "Installing it restarts Mentor (about a minute)".
- Update opens a question, "Restart to update?", with "Not now" and "Restart and update"; only the second starts mentor-update-apply.service.
- Mentor Settings, Updates asks the same question before its Update button does anything.

Changed: mentor-chat (the card and the question), mentor-settings (the same question), UPDATES.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor update` names this version 15.10.26.03.
- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
