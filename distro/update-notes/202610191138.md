# Mentor update: the open-app line is seen on a light taskbar

## What is new

- With light window colors, the line under each open app on the taskbar is dark, and the app in front has a faint dark shade, so both can be seen. Dark sets keep the light line.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19: on a light taskbar the line could not be seen. The extension's part `taskbar color` now sets Dash to Panel's running-indicator colors (`dot-color-*`, `dot-color-override`) and the focus shade (`focus-highlight-color`, `focus-highlight-opacity`) on a light tone, and resets them otherwise while they are Mentor's. DESKTOP.md.

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: it loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- In a light set, `gsettings get org.gnome.shell.extensions.dash-to-panel dot-color-1` says `'#3d3d3d'`.
