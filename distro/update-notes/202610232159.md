# Mentor update: Themes in the desktop menu, and mixed window colors

## What is new

A right click on the desktop now has **Themes** where "Change Background…" was. It opens Mentor Settings on its Themes tab. The tab has a new group of window colors called **Mixed**: light windows in a dark frame (dark title bars, side panels and taskbar), or dark windows in a light frame. The desktop picture can be changed from the same tab, under **Background**.

## Why

The user asked for Themes in the right-click menu in place of Change Background, and for themes that mix light and dark parts. Changed files: `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat` (the menu: action `win.themes` runs `mentor-settings themes`), `/usr/local/bin/mentor-settings` (the Mixed group and the Background row), `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor_colors.py` (`MIXED_SETS`, `FRAME_TEXT`, `ALL_PALETTES`, `is_mixed`, `kind`), `/usr/local/bin/mentor` (`mentor colors` lists them as `mixed`), and the shell extension (`extension.js`, part `taskbar color`, and `stylesheet-light.css` / `stylesheet-dark.css`: the taskbar's text is light or dark by the taskbar's own tone). DESKTOP.md "Window colors", "Mixed sets" explains it all. The extension's change shows after the restart that comes with this update.

If the user says a mixed set's taskbar text cannot be read: `cat ~/.config/mentor/taskbar-color` names the set's tones, and the extension's report (`gdbus call --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell --method org.mentor.Shell.Status`) must say `"taskbar color":"ok"`. Programs that are open take a new set when they are opened again.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- `mentor colors` lists six sets marked `mixed` (Paper and ink, Cream and coffee, Mist and navy, Sage and forest, Night and mist, Charcoal and sand).
- A right click on the desktop shows Hide chat, New conversation | Report a problem… | Themes, Mentor Settings…, Settings.
