# Mentor update: one pointer at any zoom

## What is new

- With a larger zoom (150%, say) and a large pointer, Mentor's window no longer shows two pointers. While the zoom would make the pointer too large, Mentor uses the next smaller size; the size you chose comes back at a smaller zoom.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19: size 48 at 150% showed two pointers. The virtual graphics card holds a pointer of at most 64 by 64 pixels; 48 × 1.5 = 72 is drawn by GNOME into the picture ("Can't handle cursor size 72x72"), and the card's last pointer stayed beside it. The extension's new part `pointer fits` (only with virtio_gpu) keeps the size times the scale within 64; the choice is kept in `~/.config/mentor/pointer-size` (`mentor_pointer`). DESKTOP.md, "Pointer".

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: it loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `gdbus call --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell --method org.mentor.Shell.Status` shows `"pointer fits":"ok"`.
