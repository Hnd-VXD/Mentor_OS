# Mentor update: "More" in the bell, and History shows what each answer answered

## What is new

- The bell at the top right no longer runs dry. Under the cards there are three buttons, **More GitHub projects**, **More apps** and **More news**, and each press brings ones you have not seen yet. The bell stays (without a number) while there is more to ask for. The list of cards scrolls, and the buttons stay in view.
- More news comes from more places: besides Arch Linux and GNOME, also Phoronix, LWN, OMG! Linux, 9to5Linux and GamingOnLinux. More GitHub projects looks wider (Wayland, GNOME extensions, Linux desktops), and More apps adds what is popular on Flathub this week.
- In History, each of Mentor's answers shows above it, whole, the words it answered. Conversations started from the bell are named after the card ("New projects on GitHub…: what can we make of it?"), not all "Review this, what can we ma…".

## Why

- mentor-advisor: once a day, beside the day's cards, it writes /var/lib/mentor/advice/more.json: up to 60 GitHub projects (six topics, 90 days, 8 stars or more), 60 applications (Flathub's new feed and its trending list) and 60 news items (older Arch and GNOME news, and the news sites in HEADLINES).
- mentor-chat: the "More" buttons (more_waiting, give_more); the cards they make are kept in ~/.config/mentor/advice.json ("extra", and "had" so nothing comes twice). No model and no network are used when a button is pressed. The bell's list scrolls. History: user_words and history_card(asked). take_advice puts the card's title first.
- A new kind, headlines (news from Linux sites), which can be turned away and brought back like the others (mentor-settings, mentor_tools).

Changed: mentor-advisor, mentor-chat, mentor-settings, mentor_tools.py, SUGGESTIONS.md, ASSISTANT.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `/var/lib/mentor/advice/more.json` is there after the next reading of the news (the first run of mentor-advisor of the day; until then the bell has no "More" buttons, which is not a fault).
