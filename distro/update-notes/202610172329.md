# Mentor update: Mentor in other languages

## What is new

- With the desktop in Greek (or any other language), Mentor's self-check no longer reports its own desktop as broken.
- After a change of language, GNOME no longer asks whether to rename your folders (Documents, Downloads...): they keep their names, so Mentor keeps finding your recipes.

## Why

- `mentor doctor` (and with it the desktop check, the self-check card of mentor-watch and the tool desktop_check) read `gnome-extensions info` for "State:"; in Greek it says "Κατάσταση:", so Mentor's extension showed as "BROKEN ... not found" and a red card could come for a desktop that works. The programs it asks now answer in English (`LC_ALL=C.UTF-8`), as does `gdctl` for the scale. extension_status's hint for a new extension goes by the exit code (2), not by English words.
- After a change of language, xdg-user-dirs-gtk (`user-dirs-update-gtk.service`, user) offers to rename the home folder's folders into it. Mentor's recipes live in `~/Documents/Mentor recipes`: after a yes they would be in "Έγγραφα" and Mentor would look in a new, empty Documents. `mentor-enable` masks the service for every user. DESKTOP.md, "Languages", "Folders".

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `systemctl --global is-enabled user-dirs-update-gtk.service` says `masked`.
- `LANG=el_GR.UTF-8 mentor doctor` ends with "Everything of Mentor's desktop is in place."
