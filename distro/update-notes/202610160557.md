# Mentor update: a tidier desktop menu, and suggestions in Mentor Settings

## What is new

The desktop's right-click menu is shorter: "Report a problem…" has its own place at the end of Mentor's part. The settings for the bell's suggestions are now a tab of Mentor Settings, which you open from your applications. Problem reports go to the Mentor repair center, and Mentor asks before sending one anonymously.

## Why

- mentor-chat: "Report a problem…" in its own section above GNOME's items; "Suggestions…" and "Mentor Settings…" left the menu; the old Suggestions window (show_advice_settings) is gone.
- mentor-settings: a Suggestions tab (on or off, which kinds, and what was turned away with Bring back); `mentor settings suggestions` opens it.
- mentor_tools: the report is offered as "Send this to the Mentor repair center anonymously?".

Changed: mentor-chat, mentor-settings, mentor_tools.py, AGENT.md, ASSISTANT.md, SETTINGS.md, SUGGESTIONS.md, UPDATES.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- Right-clicking the desktop shows Hide chat, New conversation, then Report a problem…, then GNOME's items.
- Mentor Settings has the tabs Settings, Suggestions and Updates.
