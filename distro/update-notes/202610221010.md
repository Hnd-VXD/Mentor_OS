# Mentor update: no zoom jump on a new window size, Uninstall for your own apps, the picture menu sooner

## What is new

- When you maximize Mentor's window or make it full screen for the first time, the desktop no longer shows small for a moment before jumping to your size (on a Windows screen scaled 200% or 300%).
- Apps you added yourself have **Uninstall** in their icon's right-click menu; Mentor then takes them away for you. What came with Mentor cannot be uninstalled.
- After Print Screen on Windows, the picture menu opens at once and fills in when the picture arrives (with the newest Mentor.exe).

## Why

- Zoom jump (the user, 2026-10-22): mutter shows every window size it has not seen yet at 100% until the shell extension sets the scale again (about 0.2 s). The extension now also sets `org.gnome.desktop.interface scaling-factor` to the wanted scale when it is whole (`_defaultScale`), which mutter uses for new sizes; with 150% it stays 0 and nothing changes. DESKTOP.md, "Sharp text".
- Uninstall: new `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-apps` and `mentor_apps.py` (which apps came with Mentor), a pacman hook `mentor-apps.hook`, the extension's app menu, the tools and policy refusing Mentor's own apps. UNINSTALL.md.
- Picture coming: `mentor-clipboard` passes Mentor.exe's `{"image-coming": true}` to the extension (`HostPictureComing`). DESKTOP.md, "The menu after a picture".
- A new Mentor.exe the same day: a loading window until the desktop is up, and a wider Mentor window (WINDOWS.md).

## Step by step

1. Nothing to do: the restart after the update loads the new shell extension, and `mentor-enable` makes the list of Mentor's own apps (`mentor-apps refresh`).

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `gsettings get org.gnome.desktop.interface scaling-factor` gives `uint32 2` on a 200% Windows screen (0 at 150% or on a real computer).
- `mentor-apps check chromium` says `mentor`.
