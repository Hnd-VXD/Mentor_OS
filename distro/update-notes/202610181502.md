# Mentor update: answers you can read over any page

## What is new

- When you ask with the box over a program (Super+Z), the answer above it has a solid background: the page behind no longer shows through it.

## Why

- The answer bubble and the box over a program were drawn a little see-through (86 %), like the cards on the desktop. Over a page of white text (Reddit in the dark style) the page showed through and the answer could not be read (the user, 2026-10-18). In `mentor-chat` the answer, the box, the picture chip and the model button are solid when they are over a program (`window.ask-over`), with a shadow; on the desktop they stay as they were. ASSISTANT.md, "Over a program".
