# Mentor update: the list of updates sorted by year and month

## What is new

In Mentor Settings, Updates, the list of updates is now sorted into years and months: open a year, then a month, to see its updates. A new Mentor also lists the updates it came with.

## Why

- mentor-settings: "Every update" is grouped by year, then month (Adw.ExpanderRow inside Adw.ExpanderRow), newest first, with counts; what was opened stays open when the list is shown again. A small style rule (NESTED_ARROW_CSS) keeps a closed month's arrow from looking open inside an open year.
- Builds now carry every update published before them: /etc/mentor/updates-included.json and their notes in /etc/mentor/update-notes/ (from distro/updates.json and distro/update-notes/ in the source). Settings lists those as "Came with this Mentor".
- build.sh copies them; publish-update.ps1 adds each published update to that list.

Changed: mentor-settings, UPDATES.md, SETTINGS.md; new: /etc/mentor/updates-included.json and the notes of 15.10.26.01 to .03.

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- Mentor Settings, Updates shows the year 2026 with its updates inside.
