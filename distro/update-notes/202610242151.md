# Mentor update: the yellow, red and green cards as frosted glass too

## What is new

The yellow warning, red problem and green idea cards in the chat now show the frosted glass as well: the wallpaper behind them comes through softly blurred, and their text stays easy to read. As before, this needs a graphics card.

## Why

Asked for by the user after 24.10.26.02. In `GLASS_CSS` (`/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat`) the `.notice.warning`, `.notice.problem` and `.notice.idea` tints go from 0.92 to 0.78. Tried over a light wallpaper covered in small black text: the blur makes it a flat haze and the cards read easily. If the user finds them hard to read, DESKTOP.md, "Frosted glass", says to raise these three back toward 0.92. Only `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat` and `/etc/mentor/DESKTOP.md` changed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- `pgrep -af bin/mentor-chat` shows the assistant running.
