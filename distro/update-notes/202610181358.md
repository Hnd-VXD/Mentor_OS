# Mentor update: smooth animations

## What is new

- Windows, the overview and menus open and close with GNOME's short animations again, drawn by the graphics card.
- With the Graphics card switch off in the Mentor window on Windows, they stay off, so the desktop does not get slow.
- If you turned animations off yourself (Settings, Accessibility, Reduce Animation), they stay off: that is your choice.

## Why

- Animations were switched off for everyone when Mentor had no 3D in the VM. Measured now with the graphics card (Radeon 860M): the window shows about 118 frames a second on a 120 Hz screen, and animating costs the shell about 12 % of one core while something moves. `00-mentor` now has `enable-animations=true`; the extension's new part `animations` holds them back for the session when no graphics card draws the screen (virtio-gpu without its 3D feature bit, or no GPU render node), as on a PC of its own without a driver. DESKTOP.md, "Animations".
