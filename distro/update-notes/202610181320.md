# Mentor update: lighter with many tabs open

## What is new

- Chromium puts tabs you have not looked at for a while to sleep, and wakes them when you click them. You can turn this off in Chromium's Settings, Performance.
- When memory runs short, Mentor first compresses what has not been used for a while, instead of closing a program.

## Why

- On the owner's Mentor, Chromium's ten tabs held about 2.5 GB and a tab in the background used 28 % of a processor. `/etc/chromium/policies/recommended/mentor.json`: `HighEfficiencyModeEnabled` true, `MemorySaverModeSavings` 1 (recommended, not enforced). DESKTOP.md, the Chromium rows.
- There was no swap at all. `zram-generator` (a new package of Mentor's list, installed by this update) makes `/dev/zram0` at every start from `/etc/systemd/zram-generator.conf` (half the memory, at most 8 GB, zstd); `/etc/sysctl.d/90-mentor-zram.conf` sets swappiness 180 and page-cluster 0. DESKTOP.md, "Memory". Works the same on a Mentor started on its own PC.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `swapon --show` lists `/dev/zram0` (after the restart that ends the update); Memory Saver works from Chromium's next start.
