# Mentor update: the menus and side windows as frosted glass too

## What is new

The assistant's menus (the bell, the model menu, the desktop's right-click menu, the list of conversations) and its windows History, Network and Important warnings now look like frosted glass in your window colors, like the chat's cards. Pictures and suggestions opened over the chat stay solid so their text stays clear. As before, this needs a graphics card.

## Why

Asked for by the user after 24.10.26.01 ("all our elements"). `GLASS_CSS` in `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat` now also gives every `popover` and the side windows (class `glass`, set in `side_window` and `show_history`) a see-through background in the set's own tones with a `backdrop-filter`. The menu of the All conversations button (an `osd` button, white text) gets the cards' dark glass: on light glass its text was white on white. Dialogs over the chat (`Adw.Dialog`) stay solid: GTK does not blur inside its own window, and the chat's text showed through them sharp. DESKTOP.md, "Frosted glass". Only `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat` and `/etc/mentor/DESKTOP.md` changed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- `pgrep -af bin/mentor-chat` shows the assistant running.
- With a graphics card, the menus and History show the wallpaper or windows behind them softly blurred, and their text is easy to read in both light and dark sets.
