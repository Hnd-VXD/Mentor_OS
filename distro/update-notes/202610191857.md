# Mentor update: pictures from Windows paste into web pages

## What is new

- A screenshot or picture copied on Windows now pastes into web pages in Chromium (a forum post, say) at any size. Before, a large one made the page stop responding.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19: pasting a screenshot into a forum froze the page ("Page Unresponsive"). The picture was held by `mentor-clipboard`, an X11 program, and GNOME does not pass a picture over about 256 KB from the X11 side to Wayland programs (gnome-shell logs `meta_x11_selection_input_stream_read_async ... finalized without ever returning`); Chromium waited for it. Now the shell holds it: `mentor-clipboard` calls the extension's new `SetClipboardImage` with the PNG it keeps. Tested with 480 KB and 4 MB. DESKTOP.md.

## Step by step

1. The extension changed: it loads at the next login. The restart that installs this update logs in again; nothing else is needed.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `gdbus introspect --session --dest org.gnome.Shell --object-path /org/mentor/Shell | grep SetClipboardImage` shows the method.
