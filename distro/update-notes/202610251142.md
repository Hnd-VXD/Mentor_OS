# Mentor update: clear words when Groq's free allowance runs out

## What is new

When Groq's free allowance for a model runs out, the assistant now says so at once, with roughly when it is back, instead of waiting minutes and then giving up. Each Groq model has its own allowance, so choosing another one in the model menu usually works right away.

## Why

`/usr/local/bin/mentor-agent` waited out every 429 the same way: a user waited 5½ minutes for a bare "used up", and which limit it was could not be told afterwards. Groq's refusal names the limit and the wait ("tokens per day (TPD): Limit 200000, Used 199870 … Please try again in 7m12s"). Now a limit a day, or a wait of more than two minutes, ends the turn at once in plain words; short waits use Groq's own time; any other refusal that outlasts the waits ends with Groq's own words in brackets. ENGINES.md, "Groq, in light mode", says the same.
