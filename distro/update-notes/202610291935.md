# Mentor update: sound for every program, and warning cards you cannot miss

## What is new

Programs that play sound the old way (some games, emulators, small tools) were silent in Mentor; now they play through your speakers like everything else. A warning card, such as "a program wants to go online", now always shows where you are looking, even when you switch to another conversation. And if a program keeps waiting for your answer and its card is gone, the card comes back.

## Why

Sound: Mentor shipped no ALSA route to PipeWire, so a program playing through ALSA itself got "Device or resource busy" and was silent. Packages `alsa-plugins` and `alsa-utils` were added to Mentor's list (the update installs them), and `/etc/alsa/conf.d/99-zz-mentor-default.conf` sends ALSA's `default` to PipeWire's PulseAudio server, falling back to the card when none runs. Not `pipewire-alsa`: with PipeWire 1.6.10 its `aplay` played and then never exited. WINDOWS.md, section 5, "Programs that play through ALSA itself".

Cards: a card was added to whichever tab was in front when it came, and a user looking at another tab never saw it (seen with two programs kept offline by the network guard). In `/usr/local/bin/mentor-chat` a card of the system's now moves to the end of the tab the user looks at until one of its buttons is used (`place_cards`, `settle_card`, `drop_card`), and `remind_unseen` brings a waiting program's card back when it tries again and no card of its own is left, at most every 15 minutes per program. Grouped notices carry a `programs` list (`mentor_tools.leave_notice`, `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-watch`). ASSISTANT.md ("A card goes with the user") and SECURITY.md ("Programs going online") say how.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- `pacman -Q alsa-plugins alsa-utils` lists both.
- As the user: `aplay -L | grep -A1 '^default'` says "through its PulseAudio server", and `timeout -s KILL 5 aplay -q /usr/share/sounds/alsa/Front_Center.wav; echo $?` plays and says `0`.
