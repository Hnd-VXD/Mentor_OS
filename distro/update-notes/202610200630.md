# Mentor update: report what the instructions cannot mend

## What is new

- When a part of Mentor stays broken for a day, a card offers to tell Mentor's maker (Report a problem), so that an update can fix it for everyone.
- When Mentor follows its own instructions for a part and that does not bring it back, it says so and offers the same report, instead of trying on and on.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-20: a new GNOME can make Mentor's instructions for a part out of date; then only an update can mend them, and the maker has to hear of it. `still_broken` in `mentor-watch` (`~/.local/state/mentor/watch-since.json`), the card's button "Report a problem"; AGENT.md, DESKTOP.md ("Mending one part"), UPDATES.md.
