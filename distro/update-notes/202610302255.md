# Mentor update: a closer look at suspicious programs, and a watch on your passwords and keys

## What is new

When Mentor flags a suspicious program, it can now look at it much more closely and tell you what it found. Mentor also tells you if an unknown program opens your passwords, keys or your browser's saved data, or changes one of Mentor's own files. And the start screen and the model menu now group NVIDIA and Groq together as "Free models".

## Why

New tool `security_investigate` (`/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-investigate`, `mentor-investigate.socket`): only for programs on the guard's lists, no password, changes nothing. YARA (`/usr/local/share/mentor/yara/mentor.yar`), capa (fetched at its first use, pinned by SHA-256) and tshark run locked up, with no network. New packages: `wireshark-cli`, `yara`. New `auditd` rules (`/etc/audit/rules.d/mentor.rules`, `mentor_audit.py`) and cards (`secret_findings`, `own_findings` in `mentor_guard.py`). `FREE_MENUS`, `show_choice` in `mentor-chat`. SECURITY.md, "A closer look at a flagged program" and "Who touched passwords and keys"; ENGINES.md for the menu.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file is as built.
- `systemctl is-active auditd mentor-investigate.socket` says `active` twice; `sudo auditctl -l` lists `mentor-secrets` and `mentor-own`.
- `mentor security` has no line "NOT WORKING".
