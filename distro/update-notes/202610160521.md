# Mentor update: tell Mentor's maker when something does not work

## What is new

When something in Mentor does not work as expected, you can now tell its maker, so a next update can fix it: right-click the desktop and choose "Report a problem…", or press "Report a problem" in Mentor Settings, Updates. Mentor shows you exactly what it would send before anything goes, and it never includes your name, your files or your conversations. When an update fixes your problem, its card tells you.

## Why

New: the tool problem_report (two steps, like recipe_share: it shows the report, and sends only after the user's yes in a new message); "Report a problem…" in the desktop's right-click menu (asks the assistant in that chat); "Problems you reported" in Mentor Settings, Updates (from ~/.local/state/mentor/reports.json); update.json may name the reports an update fixes (`fixes`), and the Mentor that sent one says so on the update's card. Reports go through the recipes site's new `problem` function into a private repository only the maker reads. UPDATES.md, "Problem reports", says how.

Changed: mentor_tools.py, mentor_hub.py, mentor_update.py, mentor-chat, mentor-settings, UPDATES.md, AGENT.md, TOOLS.md, SETTINGS.md, ASSISTANT.md.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- Right-clicking the desktop shows "Report a problem…".
- Mentor Settings, Updates shows "Problems you reported".
