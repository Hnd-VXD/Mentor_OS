# Mentor update: one Update button

## What is new

- When you press Update in Mentor Settings, the update card in the chat shows "Updating…" too, and the other way round, so it is never pressed twice.
- When a newer update arrives, the card of the older one goes away: the newest always holds everything before it.

## Why

- The card in the chat and Mentor Settings, Updates each had an Update button that knew nothing of the other (the user saw both still offering Update). Both now watch `/var/log/mentor/updates.log`, which the install writes to as it starts: `mentor_update.apply_stage()` says "installing" or "failed", and both show the same. UPDATES.md, "Two Update buttons, one install".
- A newer update's card left the older card in the chat; its button installed the newest anyway. The chat keeps one update card (`update_card` on the window).

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
