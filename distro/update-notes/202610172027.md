# Mentor update: one volume for Windows and Mentor

## What is new

- Your laptop's volume keys, Windows' volume and Mentor's volume are now one and the same. Before, they added up: 22% on Windows and 70% in Mentor sounded like 15%. Mute is shared too.
- This needs the newest Mentor program on Windows, which updates itself. With an older one, Mentor's volume works as before.

## Why

- Windows' master volume and Mentor's own volume multiplied. Now Mentor.exe (WindowsVolume.cs) sends Windows' volume and mute over the link, and `mentor-host-link` starts `/usr/local/lib/mentor/mentor-volume` as the desktop's user: apps play to a virtual output, "Speakers" (`mentor_speakers`), whose volume only shows Windows' and does not lower the sound; a loopback passes the sound to the real output at 100%. Changes to Mentor's volume go back to Windows. When Mentor.exe goes away, the real output is the default again at the same volume. WINDOWS.md, section 5, "One volume".
- If the user says there is no sound since this update: `pactl list short sinks` must show the real output at 100% and `mentor_speakers` as the default while Mentor.exe is connected; WINDOWS.md says what to do.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- With the newest Mentor.exe: `pactl get-default-sink` says `mentor_speakers`, and `pactl get-sink-volume mentor_speakers` shows the same percentage as Windows' volume.
