# Mentor update: a guard against harmful programs

## What is new

- **A watch for unusual programs.** Every ten minutes Mentor looks at what runs and what starts by itself, for the signs harmful programs give: mining cryptocurrency, a name made to look like the system's own, a program in a temporary folder, an unknown program working hard in the background, something new that starts by itself. A card says what it found; with "Let Mentor look by itself at what seems wrong" on, Mentor looks at once and tells you in a few words.
- **A card the first time an unknown program goes online.** Mentor Settings has a new tab, **Security**: what is blocked from the internet (each program once, with where it tried to go, and Unblock), and unknown programs that went online (Block, Ask Mentor). Nothing is blocked without your yes.
- **No programs from temporary folders**: a program dropped into /tmp cannot start at all.
- **A record of every program started**, so that even one that ran for a few seconds is noticed.
- **A weekly check of this system's programs**, when you agree: a green card with Check now.
- A shutdown or restart waits at most 30 seconds for a program that does not stop.

## Why

- The user, 2026-10-19/20, after seeing an AI agent find a cryptominer on someone's Mac. SECURITY.md: "Unusual programs", "No programs from temporary folders", "Programs going online", "The weekly check of this system's programs". The guard on programs going online is OpenSnitch's daemon (package `opensnitch`, added by this update) with Mentor as its interface (`mentor-netguard`). New: `mentor_guard.py`, `mentor-guard`, `mentor-netguard`, `mentor-execlog`, `mentor-noexec`, their services, `mentor check-files`. DESKTOP.md "Shutdown" for the 30 seconds.

## Step by step

1. The package `opensnitch` is added by the update, and `mentor-enable` switches the new services on. The restart that installs the update starts them all; nothing else is needed.
2. The watch's first look only learns what is usual on this computer: nothing is flagged by it.

## Check at the end

- `mentor verify` says every file of Mentor's is as it was built.
- `systemctl is-active mentor-noexec mentor-execlog mentor-netguard opensnitchd mentor-guard.timer` says active five times, and `mentor security` has no line "NOT WORKING".
- `findmnt -no OPTIONS --target /tmp` has `noexec`.
- `curl -sI https://archlinux.org | head -1` still answers: the internet never depends on the guard.
